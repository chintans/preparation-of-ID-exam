# Syllabus & Topic Status

Built from the official IICA scheme. The test covers four areas: **Companies Law, Securities Law, Basic Accountancy, Corporate Governance** (including case studies). It has 50 MCQs (100 marks) in 75 minutes, split between knowledge-based ("Board Essentials") and scenario-based ("Board Practice") questions. The pass mark is 50% and there is no negative marking.

Status key: `not started` · `learning` · `needs revision` · `strong`
