| Q | Ans | Topic | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C | A3 | Rule 6(4) as amended on 18 Dec 2020: the test must be passed within 2 years of inclusion in the databank, with at least 50% in aggregate. The old rule was 1 year and 60%. |
| 2 | B | A3 | 3+ years as KMP of an unlisted public company with paid-up capital ≥ ₹10 crore qualifies. A: CS practice needs 10 years. C: private companies don't count. D: overlapping periods count once, so the total is 2 years. |
| 3 | C | A2 | Rule 4(1): paid-up ≥ ₹10 crore, turnover ≥ ₹100 crore, or loans/debentures/deposits **exceeding** ₹50 crore. Exactly ₹50 crore doesn't exceed it, so no test is met. |
| 4 | A | B1 | LODR Reg 17(1)(b): with no regular non-executive chairperson (here the chair is the MD), at least half the board must be IDs. ½ × 10 = 5. |
| 5 | D | A2/B1 | 1/3 × 7 = 2.33, rounded up to 3 (Explanation to Sec 149(4)). LODR also requires 1/3 for a non-promoter non-executive chair. |
| 6 | D | A2 | Rule 4(1) proviso: unlisted JVs, WOSs and dormant companies are exempt. An 80% subsidiary isn't a WOS. |
| 7 | A | A1 | Sec 149(6)(e)(ii)(A): a partner in the statutory audit firm of the company or its holding/subsidiary/associate company, in any of the 3 preceding FYs. FY 2024-25 falls within the 3 FYs before FY 2026-27. |
| 8 | C | A1 | Sec 149(6)(c): up to 10% of the director's own total income. The 2% of company turnover limit is the relatives' test in 149(6)(d). |
| 9 | B | A1 | Proviso to Sec 149(6)(e)(i) (2017 amendment): a relative's employment doesn't disqualify. A relative who was KMP would. |
| 10 | D | B1 | LODR Reg 16(1)(b)(viii): at least 21 years old. |
