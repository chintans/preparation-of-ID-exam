## A5: Declarations and Schedule IV (Sec 149(7), (8); Rule 6(3); Reg 25)

- **Declaration timing (Sec 149(7)):** first board meeting attended as a director; **first board meeting of every financial year**; and whenever circumstances change.
- **Also declare** databank compliance (Rule 6(3)). Listed: no circumstance impairs objectivity (Reg 25(8)); board records it **after assessing veracity** (Reg 25(9)).
- **Separate meeting (Schedule IV Part VII; Reg 25(3)):** at least **once a financial year**, **no non-IDs and no management**. It **reviews** (non-IDs, board, chair, information flow). It **decides nothing**.
- **Evaluation (Part VIII):** by the **entire board minus the ID being evaluated**; result drives reappointment (Part V).
- **Listed extras:** familiarisation programme (Reg 25(7)); D&O insurance for **top 1,000** by market cap (Reg 25(10)).

## A7: Liability (Sec 149(12), 2(60), 463)

An ID (and a non-promoter, non-KMP NED) is liable **only** for company acts that happened:
1. with their **knowledge, attributable through board processes**; or
2. with their **consent or connivance**; or
3. where they **had not acted diligently**.

Shields: **recorded dissent** in the minutes; reading papers and asking questions; Sec 463 relief for acting **honestly and reasonably**; MCA General Circular 01/2020 tells ROCs to check board records before proceeding against IDs.

**The one-line test:** "Did it reach me through board papers or meetings, and did I either agree, stay silent, or fail to dig?" If yes, no shield.
