# Session 4: Recap quiz (27 Oct 2026)

6 MCQs, about 7 minutes, closed-book. Write your letters in `answers.md`.

**Q1.** Ms Iyer served two consecutive terms as an independent director of Rho Ltd, ending 31 Mar 2025. In June 2026 Rho Ltd wants to hire her as a paid strategy consultant. Which statement is correct?
- A) Allowed, because the cooling-off only bars reappointment as an ID
- B) Allowed, if shareholders pass a special resolution
- C) Not allowed: it breaches the 3-year cooling-off under Sec 149(11)
- D) Allowed, because one year has passed since her term ended

**Q2.** Sigma Ltd (listed) proposes a new independent director. Overall votes: 68% for, 32% against. Among public shareholders: 45 lakh votes for, 55 lakh against. What is the outcome?
- A) Not appointed
- B) Appointed, because a simple majority is enough under the Companies Act
- C) Deemed appointed under the proviso to Reg 25(2A)
- D) Appointed, if the board ratifies within 3 months

**Q3.** Under Sec 149(7), when must an independent director give a declaration of independence?
- A) At every board meeting
- B) At the first board meeting attended as a director, at the first board meeting of every financial year, and when circumstances affecting independence change
- C) Only at the time of first appointment
- D) At every annual general meeting

**Q4.** Tau Ltd has no MD, WTD or manager. Its net profit under Sec 198 is Rs 20 crore. What is the maximum total commission it may pay all its non-executive directors without a special resolution?
- A) Rs 20 lakh
- B) Rs 2.2 crore
- C) Rs 1 lakh per meeting
- D) Rs 60 lakh

**Q5.** Which independent director is most likely to be held liable under Sec 149(12)?
- A) An audit committee member who saw the auditor flag the same unreconciled balances for two years and never raised it
- B) An ID who recorded dissent in the minutes when the transaction was approved
- C) An ID appointed six months after the default took place, with no board papers on it
- D) An ID on approved leave who received no papers on the item

**Q6.** A listed company has 11 directors. Its chairperson is non-executive and is the brother of the promoter. What is the minimum number of independent directors under LODR Reg 17(1)(b)?
- A) 4
- B) 5
- C) 6
- D) 11
