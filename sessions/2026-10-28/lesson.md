## Sec 149 on one screen

Most of what you have studied so far (A1 to A7) sits inside one section. If you can rebuild this table from memory, you have the core of the Companies Law ID questions.

| Sub-section | What it says | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| 149(1) | Minimum 3 directors (public), 2 (private), 1 (OPC); maximum 15 (more by special resolution); woman director for prescribed classes | A8 (next) |
| 149(3) | At least one director resident in India for **182 days or more** in the financial year | A8 (next) |
| 149(4) + Rule 4 | Listed: at least **1/3** IDs. Unlisted public: **2** IDs if paid-up ≥ Rs 10 cr, **or** turnover ≥ Rs 100 cr, **or** loans + debentures + deposits **> Rs 50 cr**. JVs, wholly owned subsidiaries and dormant companies are exempt | A2 |
| 149(6) + Rule 5 | Independence tests: integrity; not a promoter; no disqualifying pecuniary relationship; limits on relatives; 3-year look-back on KMP, employee and audit-firm links; under 2% voting power | A1 |
| 149(7) | Declaration of independence: first board meeting as director, first board meeting of **every FY**, and on any change | A5 |
| 149(8) | Follow the Code in **Schedule IV** | A5 |
| 149(9) | No stock options. Sitting fees, expenses and profit-linked commission are allowed | A6 |
| 149(10) | Term of up to 5 years; a 2nd term needs a **special resolution** | A4 |
| 149(11) | Maximum 2 consecutive terms, then a **3-year** cooling-off with no association in any capacity | A4 |
| 149(12) | Liability only through knowledge via board processes, consent or connivance, or lack of diligence | A7 |
| 149(13) | IDs do **not** retire by rotation (Sec 152(6)) | A4 |
| Sec 150 + Rule 6 | Databank registration; pass the proficiency test **within 2 years** of inclusion unless exempt | A3 |

**Common exam trap:** borrowings must **exceed** Rs 50 crore. Exactly Rs 50 crore does not trigger Rule 4, while paid-up capital and turnover trigger at "**≥**".
