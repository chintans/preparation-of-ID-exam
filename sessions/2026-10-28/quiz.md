**Q1.** Kappa Ltd is an unlisted public company (not a JV, wholly owned subsidiary or dormant company). Its paid-up capital is Rs 6 crore and its turnover is Rs 95 crore. It has debentures of Rs 30 crore and a term loan of Rs 20 crore outstanding. Must it appoint independent directors under Rule 4?
- A) Yes, because its borrowings reach Rs 50 crore
- B) No, because none of the Rule 4 thresholds is crossed
- C) Yes, because its turnover is close to Rs 100 crore
- D) Yes: at least 1/3 of its board must be IDs

**Q2.** Mr Bose is proposed as an independent director of Lambda Ltd. His brother has worked at Lambda Ltd as a junior engineer (an employee, not a KMP) for the last four years. Under Sec 149(6)(e)(i), what is the effect of his brother's job on Mr Bose's independence?
- A) He is not independent, because a relative is an employee
- B) He is independent only if shareholders pass a special resolution
- C) His brother's employment does not by itself affect his independence
- D) He is independent only after his brother leaves and 3 years pass

**Q3.** Ms Desai's name was included in the ID databank on 1 Feb 2026. She does not qualify for any exemption. By when must she pass the online proficiency self-assessment test?

- B) Within 2 years of inclusion, or her name is removed from the databank
- C) Before her first appointment as an independent director, with no other deadline

**Q4.** Under Schedule IV (Part VIII) and LODR Reg 17(10), who evaluates the performance of an independent director?

- B) The nomination and remuneration committee alone
- C) The entire board, excluding the director being evaluated
