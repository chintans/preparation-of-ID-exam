## Doubts raised
1. **What is a pecuniary relationship?** It means financial dealings with the company, its holding, subsidiary or associate company, or their promoters or directors, other than director's remuneration. Sec 149(6)(c) allows up to 10% of the ID's own total income, tested over the 2 preceding FYs plus the current FY. Under Rule 5, sitting fees, reimbursements and approved commission are excluded. The 2% / ₹50 lakh limit in 149(6)(d) is the relatives' test, not the ID's own.
2. **What is KMP?** Sec 2(51) lists the CEO/MD/Manager, CS, WTD, CFO, any officer up to one level below the directors whom the Board designates, and prescribed officers. Under Sec 203 + Rule 8, listed companies and public companies with paid-up capital ≥ ₹10 cr must have whole-time KMP. KMP matters for IDs in two places: independence (a relative who was KMP disqualifies) and the Rule 6 exemption (3 years as KMP).

## Misconceptions
- Added up overlapping director and KMP service toward the 3-year exemption. Overlapping periods count once.
- Applied the 1/3 rule with an executive chair (MD). LODR requires 1/2 when the chair is executive or a promoter (or related to one).
