# Session 1 Quiz – 20 Oct 2026

10 MCQs, about 12 minutes. Try them without notes, then write your answers in `answers.md` (e.g. `Q1: B`).

**Q1.** Under Rule 6(4) of the AQD Rules (as amended), an independent director who is not exempt must pass the online proficiency test:
- A) within 1 year of databank inclusion, scoring at least 60%
- B) within 1 year of appointment, scoring at least 50%
- C) within 2 years of databank inclusion, scoring at least 50% in aggregate
- D) within 2 years of appointment, scoring at least 60%

**Q2.** Which of these people is **exempt** from the proficiency test? (Assume service is counted on the date of inclusion in the databank.)
- A) A company secretary who has practised for 8 years
- B) A person who was KMP of an unlisted public company with ₹12 crore paid-up capital for 4 years
- C) A person who was director of a private company with ₹100 crore paid-up capital for 6 years
- D) A person who was director of a listed public company for 2 years and, during the same 2 years, KMP of another listed company

**Q3.** PQR Ltd is an unlisted public company. Its latest audited financial statements show paid-up capital of ₹6 crore, turnover of ₹95 crore, and outstanding loans and debentures of exactly ₹50 crore. Must it appoint independent directors?
- A) Yes, because outstanding borrowings meet the ₹50 crore threshold
- B) Yes, because turnover is close to ₹100 crore
- C) No, because none of the Rule 4 thresholds is met
- D) Yes, one-third of its board must be independent

**Q4.** A listed company has 10 directors. Its chairperson is also its Managing Director. Under SEBI LODR Reg 17(1)(b), what is the minimum number of independent directors?
- A) 5
- B) 4
- C) 3
- D) 6

**Q5.** A listed company has 7 directors and a non-executive chairperson who is neither a promoter nor related to one. What is the minimum number of independent directors?
- A) 2
- B) 4
- C) 5
- D) 3

**Q6.** Which of the following unlisted public companies, each with paid-up capital of ₹20 crore, is **not** required to appoint independent directors under Rule 4?
- A) A company with over 2,000 shareholders
- B) A company that is an 80% subsidiary of a listed company
- C) A company with turnover of ₹500 crore
- D) A wholly owned subsidiary of another public company

**Q7.** Ms A is a partner in a CA firm that was statutory auditor of XYZ Ltd's **associate company** in FY 2024-25. In FY 2026-27, XYZ Ltd proposes to appoint her as an independent director. Is she eligible?
- A) No, because the firm was auditor of an associate company within the 3 immediately preceding FYs
- B) Yes, because the restriction applies only to the auditor of the company itself
- C) Yes, because the restriction covers only the immediately preceding FY
- D) No, because a chartered accountant can never be an independent director

**Q8.** Under Sec 149(6)(c), an independent director may have a pecuniary relationship or transactions with the company (beyond director's remuneration) only if these do not exceed:
- A) 2% of the company's gross turnover
- B) ₹50 lakh in any financial year
- C) 10% of the director's total income
- D) 25% of the director's total income

**Q9.** Dr D's daughter is a junior employee (not a KMP) of LMN Ltd. Can Dr D be appointed as an independent director of LMN Ltd, assuming every other condition is met?
- A) No, because a relative's employment in the 3 preceding FYs disqualifies her
- B) Yes, because the proviso to Sec 149(6)(e)(i) excludes a relative's employment (other than as KMP) from this restriction
- C) Only if the daughter resigns before the appointment
- D) Only with the approval of the Central Government

**Q10.** Under SEBI LODR Reg 16(1)(b), the minimum age for an independent director of a listed entity is:
- A) 18 years
- B) 25 years
- C) 30 years
- D) 21 years
