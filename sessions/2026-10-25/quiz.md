# Session 2 Quiz (25 Oct 2026)

10 MCQs, about 12 minutes, closed-book. Write your answers in `answers.md` (e.g. `Q1: B`). Q9–Q10 revisit Session 1 weak spots.

**Q1.** Ms Rao served as an ID of Alpha Ltd from 1 Apr 2016 to 31 Mar 2021 and was reappointed by special resolution until 31 Mar 2026. Which statement is correct?
- A) She can be reappointed from 1 Apr 2026 by another special resolution
- B) She can be reappointed from 1 Apr 2029, provided she wasn't associated with Alpha in any capacity during Apr 2026 to Mar 2029
- C) She can be reappointed from 1 Apr 2029 even if she was Alpha's paid consultant in the meantime
- D) She can never again be an ID of Alpha

**Q2.** Gamma Ltd, a public company, has 9 directors, 3 of whom are independent. For retirement by rotation under Sec 152(6):
- A) At least 6 directors are rotational, because the base is all 9 directors
- B) IDs are excluded from the base, so at least 4 of the 6 non-IDs must be rotational
- C) IDs retire by rotation like other directors
- D) Every director except the MD retires by rotation

**Q3.** A listed company wants to reappoint an ID for a second 5-year term. What approval is required?
- A) Board resolution only
- B) Ordinary resolution of shareholders
- C) Special resolution of shareholders
- D) Approval of the nomination and remuneration committee only

**Q4.** In a listed company's vote on appointing a new ID, 70% of all votes are in favour and 30% against. Public shareholders cast 6 crore votes in favour and 4 crore against. Under LODR Reg 25(2A), the outcome is:
- A) The resolution fails; the ID is not appointed
- B) The ID is deemed appointed under the alternate mechanism
- C) It passes as an ordinary resolution, which is enough
- D) The matter goes to SEBI for a decision

**Q5.** An unlisted public company wants to remove an ID who is in their second term. Under Sec 169, it needs:
- A) An ordinary resolution with special notice
- B) A special resolution, after giving the ID a reasonable opportunity of being heard
- C) A board resolution

- A) 7

**Q7.** Under Sec 149(7), an ID must declare that they meet the independence criteria:
