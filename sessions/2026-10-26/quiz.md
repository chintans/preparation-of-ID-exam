# Session 3 quiz (26 Oct 2026): 8 MCQs, about 10 minutes

Closed-book. Write your letters in `answers.md`. Q1–Q6 cover today; Q7–Q8 revisit your Session 1 slips.

**Q1.** What is the maximum sitting fee a company may pay an independent director for one board meeting?
- A. Rs 50,000
- B. Rs 1,00,000
- C. Rs 2,00,000
- D. There is no limit if members approve it

**Q2.** Which of these can an independent director **not** receive?
- A. Sitting fees
- B. Reimbursement of expenses for attending board meetings
- C. Stock options
- D. Profit-related commission approved by members

**Q3.** Rho Ltd has a managing director. Its net profit under Sec 198 is Rs 50 crore. What is the most it can pay all its non-executive directors together as commission **without** a special resolution?
- A. Rs 50 lakh
- B. Rs 1.5 crore
- C. Rs 5.5 crore
- D. Rs 5 crore

**Q4.** Sigma Ltd has inadequate profits this year, and its effective capital is Rs 80 crore. Under Schedule V, what is the most it can pay one independent director for the year, apart from sitting fees and without a special resolution?
- A. Rs 12 lakh
- B. Rs 17 lakh
- C. Rs 24 lakh
- D. Nil; IDs can receive only sitting fees in a year of inadequate profits

**Q5.** Ms Pillai, an ID of Tau Ltd, voted against a loan to a promoter entity at a board meeting, and her dissent is recorded in the minutes. The loan is later held to breach the Act. What is her position?
- A. Liable, because every director is an officer in default
- B. Not liable, because there was no consent or connivance and she acted diligently (Sec 149(12))
- C. Liable, because she knew about the loan
- D. Liable unless she resigned within 30 days

**Q6.** Which of these is **not** a ground under Sec 149(12) for holding an ID liable?
- A. The act happened with their knowledge, attributable through board processes
- B. The act happened with their consent or connivance
- C. They had not acted diligently
- D. They were a director of the company when the offence took place

**Q7.** *(revision: Rule 6 exemption)* Mr Sen was KMP of a listed company from Jan 2021 to Dec 2022, and a director of another listed company from Jul 2022 to Jun 2023. Is he exempt from the proficiency test under the 3-year route?
- A. Yes; the two roles add up to 3 years
- B. Yes; the two roles add up to 3.5 years
- C. No; the overlap counts once, so he has only 2.5 years
- D. Yes; any role in a listed company is enough

**Q8.** *(revision: LODR Reg 17(1)(b))* Upsilon Ltd, a listed company, has 10 directors. Its chairperson is non-executive and is neither a promoter nor related to one. What is the minimum number of independent directors?
- A. 3
- B. 4
- C. 5
- D. 2
